# review-suggestions

Adds at most five non-blocking P2 suggestions on maintainability, design and hygiene.

**Ask for it like this**

- "Any maintainability suggestions?"
- "Review it and tell me what could be cleaner."

**Boundaries**

- P2 only: it can never change the decision or lower a P0/P1.
- Style taste and linter-owned issues are dropped.

The contract is [`skills/review-suggestions/SKILL.md`](../../skills/review-suggestions/SKILL.md); this page
only orients. Back to the [feature index](README.md).
